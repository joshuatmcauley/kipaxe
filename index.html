Questions

THE USUAL ONES

How much does a website cost?

One-page sites start at £300. Bigger sites, booking systems and online ordering are quoted per project, so you know the full price before anything starts. Hosting and support is then a separate package from £150 a year.

How long does it take?

A one-page site is usually ready in about three working days once I have your photos and details. Larger builds take longer, and I'll give you a realistic timeline before you commit to anything.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Send me photos, your prices and your opening hours, and I'll handle the domain, hosting, security and everything else. If you want to update things yourself later, I'll show you how.

Can you work with what I already have?

Usually, yes. I've built sites that sit alongside existing systems for stock, payments and bookings rather than replacing them. Tell me what you're using and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth keeping.

Do you work with businesses outside Northern Ireland?

Yes. I'm based in Ballymoney and happy to meet local clients in person, but most of the work happens over messages and email, so location isn't a barrier.
